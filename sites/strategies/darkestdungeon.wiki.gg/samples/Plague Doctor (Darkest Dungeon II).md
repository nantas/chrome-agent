**Plague Doctor** is a back rank support [hero](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) with skills that focus on [DOTs](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/DOT_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), healing, and negative [tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens).

## Skills and Paths

The Plague Doctor starts the game equipped with![Dd2 ability noxiousblast.png](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_ability_noxiousblast.png/40px-Dd2_ability_noxiousblast.png?227c39) [Noxious Blast](#Noxious_Blast),![Dd2 ability blindinggas.png](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_ability_blindinggas.png/40px-Dd2_ability_blindinggas.png?d31594) [Blinding Gas](#Blinding_Gas),![Dd2 ability incision.png](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_ability_incision.png/40px-Dd2_ability_incision.png?a9693c) [Incision](#Incision),![Dd2 ability battlefieldmedicine.png](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_ability_battlefieldmedicine.png/40px-Dd2_ability_battlefieldmedicine.png?735d02) [Battlefield Medicine](#Battlefield_Medicine), and![Dd2 ability ounceofprevention.png](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_ability_ounceofprevention.png/40px-Dd2_ability_ounceofprevention.png?15d37c) [Ounce of Prevention](#Ounce_of_Prevention). ## Trivia

- The Plague Doctor is one of the twelve [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) returning from [Darkest Dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon), and one of the first nine [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) to be available in the sequel. [[1]](#cite_note-PC_Gamer-1)
- The Plague Doctor is the only hero who can directly apply all![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) types with her skills.
- The default name of the Plague Doctor, [Paracelsus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracelsus), was also one of the names of a 16th century Swiss physician who helped birth modern toxicology.
