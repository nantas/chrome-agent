The **Duelist** is a hero in *[Darkest Dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)*, exclusive to![Exclusive to The Fire's Edge DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_burn.png/20px-Poptext_burn.png?af0b4e) [The Fire's Edge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Fire%27s_Edge) DLC. [[1]](#cite_note-1) Enabling the Duelist will increase the [hero roster size](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stage_Coach#Hero_Barracks) by 1.

## Unique Attributes

The Duelist has two stances, [Defensive](#Defensive_Stance) and [Aggressive](#Aggressive_Stance), each with its own set of skills. She begins expeditions in Defensive stance, and her skill *[Anticipation](#Anticipation)* can be used to switch to the opposite stance. *Anticipation* is a [free action](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon)#Free_Actions), allowing the Duelist to use another skill immediately afterwards.

The Duelist's current stance persists between battles, unlike the [Abomination](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Abomination_(Darkest_Dungeon)) who reverts to human form at the end of battle.

Additionally, upon starting battle, the Duelist will activate![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_riposte.png/20px-Poptext_riposte.png?85a71b) [Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Riposte) for 2 rounds, and apply a -50% Riposte DMG debuff to herself for 1 round. This debuff can be removed by [Medicinal Herbs](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Medicinal_Herbs).

The Duelist's seven skills are divided between her two stances: each stance has three unique skills, with *[Anticipation](#Anticipation)* being shared between them. Though *Anticipation* is a single skill, it has different effects for each stance, so it is listed as two separate skills in the sections below for better readability.

### Notes

- All buffs and debuffs last for 3 rounds unless otherwise specified.
- Unlike most heroes, the Duelist can have all seven skills equipped at the same time, and newly recruited Duelists will have all their skills unlocked.
- The Duelist's![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_riposte.png/20px-Poptext_riposte.png?85a71b) [Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Riposte) attack has a base accuracy of 100 and 4% base critical chance.
- *[Anticipation](#Anticipation)* is a [free action](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon)#Free_Actions). It also has a one-turn cooldown, so it cannot be used immediately afterwards to return ot the previous stance.
- *[Coup de Grâce](#Coup_de_Grâce) '* s "On Kill" effect does not trigger when destroying [corpses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse). (This is consistent with other "On Kill" effects.)
- The extra action from *Coup de Grâce* counts as a full turn. Notable interactions include:
  - [Status effects](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects_(Darkest_Dungeon)) like buffs and damage over time will trigger and decrement.
  - Triggering an extra turn will reset *Anticipation'* s cooldown. In particular, using *Anticipation* is the primary way to maintain her![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_riposte.png/20px-Poptext_riposte.png?85a71b) [Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Riposte) after the extra turn since it would otherwise expire.
  - Killing an enemy with *Coup de Grâce*, then attacking again, counts as two active attacks for the purposes of [stalling](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stalling).
