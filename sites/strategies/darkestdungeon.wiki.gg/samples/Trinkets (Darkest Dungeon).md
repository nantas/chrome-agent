**Trinkets** are pieces of equipment obtained while adventuring in the dungeons, earned as rewards from quests, or bought at the [Nomad Wagon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Nomad_Wagon). Certain trinkets are earned by defeating specific enemies. Each hero is able to carry up to two different trinkets at a time. If a hero dies, their trinkets can be scavenged if the player perseveres and wins the battle. Fleeing a battle will not give such an opportunity. The trinkets confer stat bonuses and penalties when worn, and it is up to the player to use them to their full effect.

Trinkets have different rarities, which are denoted in game by color and description. A trinket is given as a reward for completing each [expedition](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Expedition), starting at Common for short Apprentice dungeons and increasing one level of rarity for each additional difficulty or length (up to Ancestral for long Champion dungeons). Boss-slaying expeditions always have a Very Rare trinket as a reward regardless of difficulty.

Some trinkets can be found as a quest reward for only a specific region. These can still be bought from the [Nomad Wagon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Nomad_Wagon).

Some unique trinkets can only be found by killing specific enemies. [Madmen](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Madman) will very rarely drop music boxes, and [The Collector](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Collector) miniboss will sometimes drop powerful head-based trinkets (though these can also be found in [Secret Rooms](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Secret_Room)). Defeating the Champion version of any of the 8 [bosses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Bosses) (from the 4 normal regions) also awards a Trophy trinket exclusive to that boss.

Ancestral trinkets, enemy trinkets (Madman, Collector, and Trophy), and some Very Rare trinkets (particularly those from DLCs) are all unique. Once you own one, you cannot find a duplicate. If a Trophy (or a Talisman of the Flame) is lost, it will automatically reappear in the inventory the next week. If any other unique trinket is sold or discarded from the inventory, it must be found again from the same source.

If you have lost (not sold/discarded) at least eight trinkets throughout your playthrough, a special quest named *Shrieker's Perch* will become available on the Estate map. This is a regular encounter with [the Shrieker](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Shrieker) that rewards eight of your lost trinkets upon completion. Certain unique trinkets, like Ancestral trinkets, can only be recovered this way if lost.

Two trinkets, the Stone of Patience and the Stone of Endurance, are currently exclusive to the Playstation 4 and Vita versions of the game.

## Equipping Trinkets

Trinkets are listed in your Trinket [Inventory](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Inventory), accessed by left-clicking the "chest" icon at the bottom right of your game screen. Your trinkets can be sorted by Class Restriction, by Rarity, or Alphabetically by name.

To equip a trinket to a Hero, open that Hero's character sheet by right-clicking the picture of that hero. Then simply drag/drop the desired trinket to one of the two slots at the bottom of the screen, or manually sorted by dragging/dropping them to a new slot in the inventory. If a trinket is already in a selected slot (on the character or in the inventory), that trinket will swap places with the new one.

This technique can also be used *during a mission* via that Inventory screen, to either equip a newly found trinket (and freeing up an inventory slot, if no equipped trinket replaces it), to unequip an unwanted trinket (assuming there is an open slot in the Inventory), or even to swap trinkets from one character to another by using both these actions.

There is also an option to "unequip all trinkets" from any and all Heroes. Newly unequipped trinkets will be listed at the bottom of the inventory until re-sorted.

### Trinket Sets

Each hero has a unique **trinket set** consisting of two hero-exclusive trinkets, and a hero who equips both will gain an additional **set bonus**. Most heroes' set trinkets can only be found in the![Exclusive to The Crimson Court DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_vampire_resist.png/20px-Poptext_vampire_resist.png?423d7b) [Courtyard](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Courtyard) and have the rarity Crimson Court. ### Fire's Edge Trinkets

![Exclusive to The Fire's Edge DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_burn.png/20px-Poptext_burn.png?af0b4e) [The Fire's Edge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Fire%27s_Edge) DLC introduces new trinket mechanics, **trigger limits** and **quest uses**:

- A trinket with a trigger limit has conditional effects (for example, "On Attack" or "On Dodge") that can only trigger a limited number of times. After reaching the trigger limit, these conditional effects will no longer trigger. However, passive effects continue to apply.
- Camping will restore a trinket's trigger limit to its initial amount.
- A trinket with quest uses can only be used for a limited number of missions. When a trinket's quest uses are exhausted, the trinket will be **destroyed**.
- A the end of a mission, a trinket's quest use counter is reduced by one if it is equipped at any point during the mission.
- Quest uses will not affect the sell price of a trinket, so a trinket with 1 quest use remaining can still be sold for full price.
- Many trinkets with trigger limits and quest uses have different artwork depending on the number of charges they have remaining.

Unlike other DLC trinkets, trinkets in the Fire's Edge DLC are not unique.

## History

When originally introduced, Tempting Goblet's rarity was Very Rare.

A new subset of ancestral items were introduced in the Fiends & Frenzy update, Ancestor's trinkets. Ancestral trinkets not belonging to the Ancestor Set were later reclassified as Very Rare trinkets. The Inhuman Bondage update added a new subset of trinkets that can be found by defeating the new enemies ( [Madman](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Madman) and [The Collector](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Collector)). With the full release of the game, Trophy Trinkets were introduced.

With the release of the PS4 and VITA ports of the game, 5 new trinkets were added to the console versions. Two of which, the Stone of Endurance and Stone of Patience, are currently available upon starting up a new game. The other three are very rarely dropped by champion level enemies.

With the release of the![Exclusive to The Crimson Court DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_vampire_resist.png/20px-Poptext_vampire_resist.png?423d7b) [The Crimson Court](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Crimson_Court), Trinket Sets and Sets bonuses were introduced to the game, alongside plenty of new Very Rare quality trinkets and rebalancing of old trinkets' effect and rarity.

The![Exclusive to The Color of Madness DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/CoM_Icon.png/20px-CoM_Icon.png?765d43) [The Color of Madness](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Color_of_Madness) added crystalline trinkets which can be bought with shards, mostly earned by completing the [Farmstead](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Farmstead) quests. The [Musketeer](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Musketeer) was also made available to all players, bringing her trinkets into the available pool. Additionally, several of the existing trinkets were rebalanced in terms of effects and rarity.
