![Exclusive to The Crimson Court DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_vampire_resist.png/20px-Poptext_vampire_resist.png?423d7b) The Crimson Court is the first DLC for *[Darkest Dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)*, released on June 19th of 2017 for PC, Mac, and Linux, and on August 22nd of 2017 for the PS4 and Vita.

## Features

- A parallel campaign taking place during the main quest which sheds light on [The Ancestor's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Ancestor) early days, including new [cutscenes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Cinematics).
- The [Flagellant](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Flagellant), a new [Hero](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes) class: a frontline healer that can apply strong bleed damage and receives buffs on low health and on [the edge of death](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Death%27s_Door). Additionally, he cannot become virtuous, and only receives one unique affliction, [Rapturous](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Rapturous). *“Awash in blood and delusion, he bears the burden of a thousand lifetimes.”*
- A new [location](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Locations) known as " [The Courtyard](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Courtyard)," which will include new curios, traps, obstacles, and a complete faction of blood-crazed foes.
- Massive, sprawling maps with locked doors, prisoner rooms, and progress saves.
- New [enemies](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies) found in the other regions.
- Heroes can be afflicted with something known as the " [Crimson Curse](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Crimson_Curse)," which turns them into a Vampire.
- 4 new, unique Courtyard [Bosses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Boss) with some of the most complex and devious mechanics yet.
- A new wandering boss known as [The Fanatic](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Fanatic), a vampire hunter-esque madman who will hunt down any of your heroes bearing [the curse](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Crimson_Curse).
- [Districts](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Districts): New prestige buildings that offer permanent, estate-wide bonuses.
- Lore-driven [trinket sets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_(Darkest_Dungeon)#Trinket_Sets) for all heroes – with set bonuses!
