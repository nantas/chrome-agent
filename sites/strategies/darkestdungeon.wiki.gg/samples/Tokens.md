Many combat status effects in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)* are represented by **tokens**, which are visible below the health bar of any combatant.

## Mechanics

- Tokens have varying stack limits, typically stacking up to three times.
- Most tokens have a limited duration of effect. The vast majority of tokens, when not consumed, expire after three turns, including the unit's current turn.
- Certain tokens are marked as Positive or Negative. Effects that apply to Positive or Negative tokens apply *only* to those in the lists below, regardless of the token's actual effects. For example,![Combo](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_combo.png/20px-Dd2_token_combo.png?115390) [Combo](#Combo) is a common token with only negative effects, but it is not considered a Negative token and thus it cannot be removed by *[Indiscriminate Science](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Plague_Doctor_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Indiscriminate_Science)*. The easiest way to determine whether a token qualifies is to look at its colour: Positive tokens are yellow and Negative tokens are blue.
  - Most Negative tokens can be resisted with![Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Debuff_dd2.png/20px-Debuff_dd2.png?1afe3e) [Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Status_Effects) resistance.
  - ![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) and![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) [Daze](#Daze) use![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Stun_dd2.png/20px-Stun_dd2.png?19a63c) [Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Stun_and_Daze) resistance instead.
  - A number of skills apply negative tokens to the user themselves, for example, *[Encore](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Jester_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Encore)*. These tokens generally cannot be resisted.
- Certain skills may **ignore** tokens. For example, the [Runaway's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Runaway_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) *[Hearthlight](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Runaway_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Hearthlight)* will hit enemies regardless of whether they are in![Stealth](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stealth.png/20px-Dd2_token_stealth.png?5aa21b) [Stealth](#Stealth). The upgraded version of *Hearthlight* will additionally ignore![Blind](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blind.png/20px-Dd2_token_blind.png?d672e8) [Blind](#Blind) on the Runaway herself.
- Skills may also **remove** tokens. A skill that removes a token will also remove its variants — for example, an attack that removes![Dodge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodge.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodge.png?7dadd5) [Dodge](#Dodge) will also remove![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) [Dodge+](#Dodge+) from the target.
- Certain skills may **steal** tokens from the target and transfer them to the skill user.
- Some tokens may **negate** other tokens — if one is applied while the other is present on the target, then both will be removed. This also means that these two tokens may never be present on a unit at the same time.
- Likewise, some skills may **invert** tokens, for example converting![Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_strength_2.png/20px-Dd2_token_strength_2.png?c5a4df) [Strength](#Strength) to![Weak](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_weak.png/20px-Dd2_token_weak.png?966c23) [Weak](#Weak) or![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/20px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) [Vulnerable](#Vulnerable) to![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/20px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215) [Block](#Block) /![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) [Block+](#Block+). The inverted token is often, but not always, the same as the negative token.

## List of Tokens

### Positive

#### ![Crit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_crit.png/35px-Dd2_token_crit.png?5bea1b) Crit

- Next applicable move is guaranteed to be a [Critical Hit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), regardless of the move's critical chance.
  - Using combat items also consumes![Crit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_crit.png/20px-Dd2_token_crit.png?5bea1b) [Crit](#Crit) tokens.
- Inverts to![Weak](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_weak.png/20px-Dd2_token_weak.png?966c23) [Weak](#Weak).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 2

#### ![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_riposte.png/35px-Dd2_token_riposte.png?90928d) Riposte

- Counter-attacks the next damaging attack received.
  - The counter-attack is defined as a special *Riposte Attack* in the unit's skill table, with its own damage, critical chance, and additional effects. Consult individual unit pages (the [Highwayman's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Highwayman_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Riposte_Attack), for example) for more information.
  - If a unit without a pre-defined *Riposte Attack* tries to copy, steal, or otherwise gain a![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_riposte.png/20px-Dd2_token_riposte.png?90928d) [Riposte](#Riposte) token (e.g. using the *Caked Palette* [trinket](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))), nothing will happen.
  - At most one![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_riposte.png/20px-Dd2_token_riposte.png?90928d) [Riposte](#Riposte) will trigger per attack. If multiple units with![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_riposte.png/20px-Dd2_token_riposte.png?90928d) are targeted by the same attack, only the frontmost![Riposte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_riposte.png/20px-Dd2_token_riposte.png?90928d) will trigger.
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Dodge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodge.png/35px-Dd2_token_dodge.png?7dadd5) Dodge

- 50% chance to dodge next attack.
- If![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) [Dodge+](#Dodge+) is also present,![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) [Dodge+](#Dodge+) tokens will be consumed first.
- Inverts to![Blind](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blind.png/20px-Dd2_token_blind.png?d672e8) [Blind](#Blind).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/35px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) Dodge+

- 75% chance to dodge next attack.
- If![Dodge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodge.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodge.png?7dadd5) [Dodge](#Dodge) is also present,![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) [Dodge+](#Dodge+) tokens will be consumed first.
- Inverts to![Blind](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blind.png/20px-Dd2_token_blind.png?d672e8) [Blind](#Blind).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/35px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215) Block

- Receive -50% DMG from next damaging attack.
- If![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) [Block+](#Block+) is also present,![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) [Block+](#Block+) tokens will be consumed first.
- Negates and inverts to![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/20px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) [Vulnerable](#Vulnerable).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/35px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) Block+

- Receive -75% DMG from next damaging attack.
- If![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/20px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215) [Block](#Block) is also present,![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) [Block+](#Block+) tokens will be consumed first.
- Negates and inverts to![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/20px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) [Vulnerable](#Vulnerable).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Guarded](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_guarded.png/35px-Dd2_token_guarded.png?9a5358) Guarded

- Next attack directed at unit is redirected towards guarding unit instead.
  - A unit may guard, or be guarded by at most one unit at a time.
  - A guarded unit may not guard another unit.
  - If a new![Guarded](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_guarded.png/20px-Dd2_token_guarded.png?9a5358) [Guarded](#Guarded) token is added which violates one of the above conditions, the old![Guarded](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_guarded.png/20px-Dd2_token_guarded.png?9a5358) will be removed.
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_strength_2.png/35px-Dd2_token_strength_2.png?c5a4df) Strength

- Next attack deals +50% DMG.
- Negates and inverts to![Weak](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_weak.png/20px-Dd2_token_weak.png?966c23) [Weak](#Weak).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 2

#### ![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/35px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) Speed

- Acts faster next round.
  - Increases the unit's![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) [Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Speed) attribute by 100 at the start of the next [round](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Rounds_and_Turns), when turn orders are calculated.
  - This means that units with![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) will (almost certainly) act before units without the token, but two units with![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) will resolve ties between themselves as usual.
  - This increase is temporary — only active at round start — and in particular, does not interact with *turn* start![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) conditions, like the *Snappy Swig* [trinket](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)).
- Negates and inverts to![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) [Daze](#Daze).
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 1

#### ![Stealth](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stealth.png/35px-Dd2_token_stealth.png?5aa21b) Stealth

- Cannot be directly targeted by most enemy skills.
  - Can still be targeted by area-of-effect attacks.
  - Can still be targeted if no other targets are available.
- Inverts to![Taunt](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_taunt.png/20px-Dd2_token_taunt.png?40b47f) [Taunt](#Taunt).
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 2

### Negative

#### ![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/35px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) Daze

- Next action will be delayed to the end of the round.
- Converts to![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) when stacked.
- ![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) [Daze](#Daze) and![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) cannot exist on a unit at the same time;![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) will always take priority.
- Negates and inverts to![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) [Speed](#Speed).
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 1

#### ![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/35px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) Stun

- Lose next action.
  - Upon recovering, gains +50%![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Stun_dd2.png/20px-Stun_dd2.png?19a63c) RES for 1 turn.
- ![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) [Daze](#Daze) and![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) cannot exist on a unit at the same time;![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](#Stun) will always take priority.
- Inverts to![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) [Speed](#Speed).
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 1

#### ![Blind](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blind.png/35px-Dd2_token_blind.png?d672e8) Blind

- 50% chance to miss next attack.
- Inverts to![Dodge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodge.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodge.png?7dadd5) [Dodge](#Dodge).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 2

#### ![Weak](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_weak.png/35px-Dd2_token_weak.png?966c23) Weak

- Next attack deals half damage
- Negates and inverts to![Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_strength_2.png/20px-Dd2_token_strength_2.png?c5a4df) [Strength](#Strength).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 2

#### ![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/35px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) Vulnerable

- Receive +50% DMG from next damaging attack.
- Negates![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/20px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215) [Block](#Block) and![Block+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blockplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_blockplus.png?e09a22) [Block+](#Block+). Inverts to![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/20px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 2

#### ![Taunt](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_taunt.png/35px-Dd2_token_taunt.png?40b47f) Taunt

- Enemies are forced to attack this unit if able.
- Inverts to![Stealth](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stealth.png/20px-Dd2_token_stealth.png?5aa21b) [Stealth](#Stealth).
- Duration: 3 turns
- Limit: 3

#### ![Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_immobilize.png/35px-Dd2_token_immobilize.png?e5a072) Immobilize

- ![Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_immobilize.png/20px-Dd2_token_immobilize.png?e5a072) [Immobilize](#Immobilize) and![Permanent Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Token_immoblize_permanent.png/20px-Token_immoblize_permanent.png?dd625a) [Permanent Immobilize](#Permanent_Immobilize) cannot exist on a unit at the same time;![Permanent Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Token_immoblize_permanent.png/20px-Token_immoblize_permanent.png?dd625a) [Permanent Immobilize](#Permanent_Immobilize) will always take priority.
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 3

#### ![Harvest Hunger](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_hunger.png/35px-Dd2_token_hunger.png?feb86a) Harvest Hunger

*Main article:* [Harvest Child](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Harvest_Child)

- *Turn start*:![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Forward 1
  - Ignores![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) [Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Move) resistance, but not![Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_immobilize.png/20px-Dd2_token_immobilize.png?e5a072) [Immobilize](#Immobilize) or![Permanent Immobilize](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Token_immoblize_permanent.png/20px-Token_immoblize_permanent.png?dd625a) [Permanent Immobilize](#Permanent_Immobilize).
- If in rank 1 after moving, forced to use [Feed the Hunger](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Harvest_Child#Harvest_Hunger).
- Duration: 1 turn
- Limit: 1
- *The hunger overwhelms all reason.*

- Only used by certain bosses or granted by [The General's Dream](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stagecoach_Items#Lair_Boss_Trophies).
- Duration:
  - Heroes: 5 rounds
  - Enemies: Until end of combat
- Limit: 1

#### [Flagellant](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Flagellant_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))

## Notes

- Damage modifiers (such as![Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_strength_2.png/20px-Dd2_token_strength_2.png?c5a4df)) stack additively on the attacker, and damage-received modifiers (![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/20px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) and![Block](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_block.png/20px-Dd2_token_block.png?de3215), e.g.) stack additively on the defender. The two cumulative modifiers are then multiplied with one another to calculate the damage amount. See also [Damage](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Damage).
- Chance to hit works similarly:![Dodge](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodge.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodge.png?7dadd5) and![Dodge+](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png/20px-Dd2_token_dodgeplus.png?352f24) both stack multiplicatively with![Blind](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_blind.png/20px-Dd2_token_blind.png?d672e8).
- ![Crit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_crit.png/20px-Dd2_token_crit.png?5bea1b) and [critical hits](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) function differently, by boosting a skill's base damage to 150% of the maximum value in its normal attack range. See [Critical Hit (Darkest Dungeon II)](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) for more details.
- Due to how![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) works by simply adding![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) 100 to that unit's initiative, its technically possible for an unit without a![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_speed.png/20px-Dd2_token_speed.png?49003d) to go before an unit with one if their![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) is high enough.
