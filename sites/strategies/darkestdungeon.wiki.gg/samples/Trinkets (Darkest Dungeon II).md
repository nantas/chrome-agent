Trinkets are treasures that can be equipped by [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) in [Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II_Wiki). Trinkets confer various bonuses or special abilities and may drastically empower a hero, but often with a cost. They can be removed or swapped when outside of combat.

**Trinket Rarity Types:**

- Vague
- Distant
- Indelible
- Unforgettable

## Hero Trinkets

Can be found randomly during the journey. Hero trinkets focus on specific archetypes and usually carry a downside that represents the burden that the trinket places on the hero.

## Trivia

- The Hag's Hoard Trinket is a reference to the [Hag](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Hag) from [Darkest Dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon).
- Most hero trinkets are references to the [Hero's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) [Shrine](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Shrine) backstories.
- The [Occultist's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Occultist_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) [Shambler's Eye](#Occultist) trinket may be dropped by the [Shambler](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Shambler_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) in lieu of another [Shambler trinket](#Shambler) if an Occultist is in the party.
- The [*Trinkets and Baubles* patch](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/19_July_2022_Patch), released in mid 2022, was a massive overhaul that changed almost every trinket in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)*.
  - The goal of the update was "to allow players to get more creative and push hero boundaries further via strategy-altering effects" as well as to ensure "that trinkets are *fun*, and thematically rich". [[1]](#cite_note-1)
  - A full list of changes may be found here: [[2]](#cite_note-2)
- The Head trinkets obtained from The Collector have details relating to the [Hero](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) they belong to.
  - The bag with Barristan's Head is tied with the [Man-at-Arms'](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Man-at-Arms_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) eyepatch, Dismas' Head is wrapped in a red cloth similar to the [Highwayman's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Highwayman_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) neckerchief, and Junia's Head has a symbol associated with the religion the [Vestal](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Vestal_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) follows.
