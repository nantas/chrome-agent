The following page is an overview of the combat system in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)*.

## Rounds and Turns

Combat takes place over a numbers of **rounds**, and each round consists of [heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) and [enemies](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) taking **turns**, or **actions**. The order in which units act is determined by their![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) [Speed](#Speed).

Normally heroes and most enemies have only one turn per round, but certain enemies (typically [bosses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Bosses_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))) may have multiple actions per round. There are also some [skills](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Skills) and [items](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) that grant extra actions. A few units do not have any actions at all, like [corpses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) or certain boss minions.

### Extra Actions

Some effects (such as [Jester's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Jester_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) Encore or the *Oversprung Pocketwatch* [trinket](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))) grant **extra actions**: they do not trigger effects that might normally trigger at the beginning or end of a turn, including![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) effects or reducing cooldowns and the duration of [status effects](#Status_Effects).

### Free Actions

Some skills, like the [Abomination's](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Abomination_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) Transform, allow the user to act after using them, usually providing some form of immediate benefit such as tokens or![Buff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_buff.png/20px-Dd2_token_buff.png?91eaf9) Buffs. A skill that grants a free action will have "Does Not End Turn" as a listed effect.

## Ranks and Size

Allied and enemy teams are each arranged into **ranks** (![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Yellow_circle.png?58b004)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Yellow_circle.png?58b004)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Yellow_circle.png?58b004)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Yellow_circle.png?58b004),![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Blue_circle.png?7723ae)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Blue_circle.png?7723ae)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Blue_circle.png?7723ae)![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Blue_circle.png?7723ae)) labelled from 1 through 4, with ranks 1 being the closest to one another and ranks 4 being the farthest apart. The vast majority of units (all heroes and most enemies) have **size** 1, meaning they occupy only one rank. However, some enemies may occupy multiple ranks. Some attacks target multiple ranks at the same time, and a unit is affected if the attack hits any of its occupied ranks. However, an attack will affect a unit once at most, regardless of how many ranks are hit. For example, a size 2 enemy will not take double damage from an attack that hits both of of its ranks.

### Health

Each unit has a certain amount of![HP](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_HP_dd2.png/20px-Icon_HP_dd2.png?1329eb) [HP](#Health) ( **Hit Points**, or **Health**) that represents how much damage they can sustain.

Most enemies will die immediately when their HP is depleted. [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), on the other hand, as well as certain enemies, will not die upon reaching![HP](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_HP_dd2.png/20px-Icon_HP_dd2.png?1329eb) 0, but will instead enter a state called![Death's Door](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Deathsdoor_dd2.png/20px-Deathsdoor_dd2.png?f98b92) [Death's Door](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Death%27s_Door_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)).

#### Fatigue

*Main article:* [Fatigue](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Fatigue)

In![Kingdoms](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Logo_kingdoms_menu.png/20px-Logo_kingdoms_menu.png?c15fa1) [Kingdoms](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Kingdoms) mode, [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) may accumulate![Fatigue](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Fatigue.png/20px-Fatigue.png?2e2dac) [Fatigue](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Fatigue) over time, which reduces their maximum![HP](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_HP_dd2.png/20px-Icon_HP_dd2.png?1329eb) [HP](#Health).

### Speed

![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) [Speed](#Speed) affects the order in which units act in combat. At the beginning of each round, a hidden *round speed* is calculated for each unit by adding their![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) to a random number from 0 to 6: the higher the round speed, the earlier the unit will act that round. [[1]](#cite_note-1) Round speed is only calculated at the beginning of a round, so a![Buff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_buff.png/20px-Dd2_token_buff.png?91eaf9) [Buff](#Status_Effects) or![Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Debuff_dd2.png/20px-Debuff_dd2.png?1afe3e) [Debuff](#Status_Effects) to![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) only takes effect during the following round.

Enemies with multiple turns per round will calculate separate round speeds for each turn, with every turn after the first receiving a hidden -2![Speed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_speed_dd2.png/20px-Icon_speed_dd2.png?3b717b) penalty compared to the previous one.

### Stress

*Main article:* [Stress (Darkest Dungeon II)](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))

![Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stress.png/20px-Dd2_token_stress.png?dec08a) [Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) is a mechanic that represents the psychological state of [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)*. Many enemy attacks inflict![Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stress.png/20px-Dd2_token_stress.png?dec08a), and![Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stress.png/20px-Dd2_token_stress.png?dec08a) may also be gained in other ways throughout the course of the expedition. Because of its increasingly negative effects at higher levels, managing![Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stress.png/20px-Dd2_token_stress.png?dec08a) is crucial to the success of the expedition.

### Resistances

Each unit has certain **Resistances**, corresponding to the many [Status Effects](#Status_Effects) that can be inflicted in combat. Resistances may be modified in many ways by skills, [items](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), and so on. Certain skills may also **pierce** resistances, negating a certain amount of the target's resistance value.

## Damage

For calculating damage, all modifiers on the attacker are added together, then all modifiers on the defender are added together, and the final damage modifier is the product of the two sums. [Critical hit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) effects are applied separately. Consider an example of a Hellion attacking with *Howling End*:

- Suppose she has 25% bonus damage from her low health passive, two trinkets granting 20% bonus damage each, a quirk granting 15% bonus damage, and a![Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_strength_2.png/20px-Dd2_token_strength_2.png?c5a4df) [Strength](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens#Strength) token. Then her damage bonus is 25% + 20% + 20% + 15% + 50% = **+130%**, or an effective damage multiplier of **2.3**.
- Suppose the enemy has a![Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_vulnerable.png/20px-Dd2_token_vulnerable.png?a66fc1) [Vulnerable](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens#Vulnerable) token. Then the enemy takes **+50%** increased damage, or a multiplier of **1.5**.
- A critical hit causes the attack to hit for the maximum value in its range, and multiplies the final damage value by **1.5**.

So the total damage dealt would be 2.3 * 1.5 * 1.5 * 18 = **93.15**.

## Critical Hits and Critical Heals

*Main article:* [Critical Hit (Darkest Dungeon II)](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))

![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/Dd2_token_crit.png?5bea1b)

A

[Crit token](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens#Crit)

Most hero or enemy skills have a chance to become a **Critical Hit**, or **Crit**, dealing more damage and healing more than usual. Critical hits also have 20% higher [resistance](#Resistances) piercing for any [Status Effects](#Status_Effects) they may apply, including damage over time, [debuffs](#Buffs_and_Debuffs), and [Movement](#Move). Heroes can also have their![Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stress.png/20px-Dd2_token_stress.png?dec08a) [Stress](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) lowered or increased depending on whether them (or an ally) has hit a critical attack or received one, respectively

## Status Effects

There are a large number of different status effects in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)*, from![Buff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_buff.png/20px-Dd2_token_buff.png?91eaf9) [buffs](#Buffs_and_Debuffs) and![Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Debuff_dd2.png/20px-Debuff_dd2.png?1afe3e) [debuffs](#Buffs_and_Debuffs) to![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)). The vast majority of status effects last for **three turns**, including the current turn; and in particular, buffs and debuffs typically need to be refreshed every other turn. Unless explicitly specified, status effects listed on the wiki should be assumed to last for three turns.

### Damage over Time

*Main article:* [Damage over Time (Darkest Dungeon II)](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))

![image](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/DoT2.png?535590)

The three types of damage-over-time effects.

There are three types of **damage-over-time** effects:![Bleed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Bleed_dd2.png/20px-Bleed_dd2.png?b226db) [Bleed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Bleed),![Blight](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Blight_dd2.png/20px-Blight_dd2.png?7e36e4) [Blight](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Blight), and![Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Burn_dd2.png/20px-Burn_dd2.png?7c3d02) [Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Burn). They are quite similar in function, each gradually reducing the unit's health over time. Certain heroes have particular affinities or weaknesses towards different types of damage over time. Different types of damage over time are also more common in certain [Regions](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Regions), and enemies within them will typically be strong in one type or another.

### Move

Many abilities![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) [Move](#Move) the user or the target. There are several different types of![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) effects:

- ![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Forward and![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Back effects move the user (or ally) towards or away from the opposing side.
- ![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Pull and![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Knockback are similar effects, but applied to enemies instead of allies.
- ![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) Shuffle moves the target to a random position.

![Move](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Move_dd2.png/20px-Move_dd2.png?f1166b) [Move](#Move) effects can be both beneficial or detrimental—combat skills require the user to be in a certain position, so moving can enable new skills to be used, or disable the moved target entirely.

### Tokens

*Main article:* [Tokens](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens)

Many status effects in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)* are represented by **Tokens**, which are visible below the health bar of any combatant. Tokens have a large number of interactions with skills, [items](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), and other tokens.

#### Stun and Daze

![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) [Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens#Stun) and![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) [Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tokens#Daze) are two special negative tokens.![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) causes the target to lose their next action, while![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) causes their action to be pushed to the very end of the round. Unlike other negative tokens, which can be resisted by![Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Debuff_dd2.png/20px-Debuff_dd2.png?1afe3e) [Debuff](#Status_Effects) resistance,![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_stun.png/20px-Dd2_token_stun.png?c42f7a) and![Daze](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_daze.png/20px-Dd2_token_daze.png?3fd45b) have their own![Stun](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Stun_dd2.png/20px-Stun_dd2.png?19a63c) [Stun](#Stun_and_Daze) resistance attribute.

### Regeneration

**![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration)** is a status effect that restores a unit's![HP](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_HP_dd2.png/20px-Icon_HP_dd2.png?1329eb) health over time. Because it is a status effect and not a direct heal, skills that grant![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) are not affected by *Healing Given* modifiers. The recipient of![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) may still benefit from *Healing Received* modifiers, but not *Healing Received from Skills*.

![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) will activate at the same time as![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), and the amount of damage taken or healed is the result of the difference between the two. For instance: if a unit has 4![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) and 6![Bleed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Bleed_dd2.png/20px-Bleed_dd2.png?b226db) [Bleed](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Bleed), they will take 2 points damage at the start of their turn, if that unit has 6![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) and 4![Blight](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Blight_dd2.png/20px-Blight_dd2.png?7e36e4) [Blight](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Blight), they will heal for 2![HP](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Icon_HP_dd2.png/20px-Icon_HP_dd2.png?1329eb) at the start of their turn, and if they have 6![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) and 6![Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Burn_dd2.png/20px-Burn_dd2.png?7c3d02) [Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)#Burn) their health won't change at all. A unit on![Death's Door](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Deathsdoor_dd2.png/20px-Deathsdoor_dd2.png?f98b92) [Death's Door](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Death%27s_Door_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) with more![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) than![Regeneration](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_regen.png/20px-Dd2_token_regen.png?943359) [Regeneration](#Regeneration) will receive a **Deathblow** check at the start of their turn

### Buffs and Debuffs

Most of the remaining status effects fall into the categories of![Buff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Dd2_token_buff.png/20px-Dd2_token_buff.png?91eaf9) **Buffs** or![Debuff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Debuff_dd2.png/20px-Debuff_dd2.png?1afe3e) **Debuffs** which help or hurt the unit, respectively. Unlike other status effects (like [tokens](#Tokens)), there is no general way to remove buffs or debuffs once applied.

## Combat Items

*Main article:* [Combat Items](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Items)

Each [hero](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) may equip a single **Combat Item**. Combat items may be used at the beginning of the hero's turn as a [free action](#Free_Actions), and have a large number of different effects, from healing and buffing allies, to damaging enemies, inflicting![Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/DoT2.png/50px-DoT2.png?535590) [Damage over Time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)), or disrupting their formation.
