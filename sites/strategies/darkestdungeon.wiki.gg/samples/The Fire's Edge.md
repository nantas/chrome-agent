![Exclusive to The Fire's Edge DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_burn.png/20px-Poptext_burn.png?af0b4e) The Fire's Edge is a [DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/DLC) for *[Darkest Dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)* released on 2026 August 18. [[1]](#cite_note-1) It adds two heroes originally introduced in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)*, a new type of [damage over time](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon)), and a number of trinkets with new and unique mechanics.

## Official Description

*Two more wayward souls wander the Old Road. The Duelist and Runaway, debuted in Darkest Dungeon II, bring their unique abilities to bear against horrors that lurk beneath the manor.* *Patterned in burns, draped in tatters and wielding a crude poker, the vagrant Runaway favours![fire](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_burn.png/20px-Poptext_burn.png?af0b4e) [fire](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon)#Burn) and![stealth](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Tray_stealth.png/20px-Tray_stealth.png?bedc3c) [stealth](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stealth_(Darkest_Dungeon)) when pressed to fight.* *By contrast, the Duelist stands severe, her ornate capelet conspicuously pierced above the breast. No challenger has yet to hold quarter against her bitter edge.*

### Features

- Two new hero classes: the [Duelist](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Duelist_(Darkest_Dungeon)) and the [Runaway](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Runaway_(Darkest_Dungeon))
- Set the dungeon alight with Runaway’s new combat mechanic:![Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_burn.png/20px-Poptext_burn.png?af0b4e) [Burn](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_over_Time_(Darkest_Dungeon)#Burn)!
- New [Trinket](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets_(Darkest_Dungeon)) Mechanics such as Trigger Uses, Quest Charges and Progressive Trinkets
- 3 new Hamlet [Districts](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Districts): Academie Duello, Craftworks, and Archaeological Base Camp

## Trivia

- *The Fire's Edge* is the first new DLC for *Darkest Dungeon* in six years. The previous DLC, [The Butcher's Circus](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Butcher%27s_Circus), was released in early 2020, and the last hero added, the![Exclusive to The Shieldbreaker DLC](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Poptext_health_damage_block.png/20px-Poptext_health_damage_block.png?16568f) [Shieldbreaker](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Shieldbreaker), was released in late 2017.
- Some voice narration from *Darkest Dungeon II* was ported back to *The Fire's Edge*. Unfortunately [Wayne June](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Wayne_June), the main voice actor for both games, passed away in early 2025.
