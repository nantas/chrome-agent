## Description

The Carrion Eater is an [enemy](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) in *[Darkest Dungeon II](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon_II)* primarily encountered in the [Creature Den](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Creature_Den) node, occasionally appearing during [Road Battles](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Road_Battle) in [The Sluice](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Sluice). It has the unique attribute of being able to evolve into the [Carrion Devourer](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Carrion_Devourer_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) after consuming a [corpse](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)).

### Notes

- **Necrophagia** only can be used during Region 2 or higher in [Confessions](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Confessions), and [Escalation](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Escalation) 2 or higher in [Kingdoms](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Kingdoms).
**Rampant Mutation** is always used during the extra turn granted by Necrophagia.

## Leucotomy

The Carrion Eater gains a unique buff if the [Chirurgeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Chirurgeon) is present. This interaction does not take place normally unless the [Corpse Light](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Flame#The_Corpse_Light) is equipped.

- Transform into [Carrion Devourer](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Carrion_Devourer_(Darkest_Dungeon_II))

## The Corpse Light

When using this flame, [corpses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)) have a 66% chance to turn into a Carrion Eater at the end of the round.
Carrion Eaters spawned this way do not leave behind [corpses](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)).
