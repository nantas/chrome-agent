A hero that traverses a dungeon will accumulate **stress**. High amounts of stress have negative effects, and if left untreated, can greatly affect their performance in combat as well as their teammates'.

## Accumulating stress

Stress is mainly gained by fighting during [expeditions](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Expedition), through monsters' critical strikes and some particular attacks that specialise in dealing stress damage. A small amount of stress is also gained when entering a [dungeon](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungeon_map) (depending on its level and the hero's [resolve](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Resolve_level)) and while travelling through it, and its magnitude is greatly influenced by the amount of [light](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Light_meter) that the adventurers have at their disposal during any stressful event. Extra stress damage is suffered if walking backwards through a corridor, when getting hit by [traps](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trap), or through some interactions with [curios](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Curio), as well as when prolonging a fight excessively. Heavy stress damage will also be applied, together with health damage, when failing to [satiate](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Hunger) a hungry party, when removing obstacles without the use of a [shovel](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Shovel), or when witnessing a comrade either reach [Death's Door](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_effects#Death's_Door) or die. Many negative [quirks](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Quirk) will increase the amount of stress suffered by a hero in certain situations, as will the drawbacks of many [trinkets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets).

Unlike combat damage, stress is not fully healed when finishing a mission. When returning to the Hamlet, stress levels are capped to 100. Stress persists on the [heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes), rendering treatment mandatory in order to make use of a stressed hero again without heavy drawbacks, and making difficult decisions such as retreating from a failing expedition more grave, as fleeing from fights and missions also stresses out the heroes.

## 100+ Stress

The stress bar is a gauge from 0 to **200**, with a threshold at 100 stress points: once this level of stress is reached, the hero will either gain an [affliction](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Affliction), or (small chance) will become [virtuous](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtue) instead. A hero with 100 or more stress must be either Virtuous or Afflicted; if something would cause their stress to exceed 100, their resolve is immediately tested.

Afflicted heroes will often disobey the player's commands and will hinder the mission by inflicting additional stress damage on themselves and the party, potentially dragging their comrades along in their path to madness. Virtuous heroes enjoy one of several possible combat buffs and will occasionally also trigger additional effects at the start of their turn depending on the virtue gained, such as healing themselves or buffing their allies. Virtuous heroes reset their Stress level to 40-45.

Upon reaching 200 stress, a character will suffer from a [heart attack](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heart_attack) and will either enter Death's Door or, if already on Death's Door, die outright. However, heroes that become virtuous will instead, upon reaching 200 stress, have their virtue removed and stress reset back to 0. See the articles on [affliction](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Affliction) and [Virtue](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtue) for more information.

## Stress Relief

Similarly to the many possible sources of stress, there are various ways of preventing and healing the stress accumulated during quests: the most typical and effective way is using two of the facilities available in the [Hamlet](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Hamlet), namely the [Tavern](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Tavern) and the [Abbey](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Abbey), which, for a cost in [gold](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold) and by holding the hero for a week, will relieve them of a large amount of stress, also ridding them of any acquired [affliction](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Affliction). A hero that is left idle in the estate will recover 5 units of stress each week, increased to 15 a week with the fairly inexpensive Puppet Theatre [District](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Districts).

It is also possible to relieve stress during an [expedition](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Expedition), by using some [camping skills](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Camping_skills) at the disposal of many heroes, or during fights by using certain abilities. Stress will also be healed when dealing critical strikes, killing enemies, receiving critical heals, disarming traps, or interacting with certain [curios](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Curio).

The player can employ a strategy where a hero can 'stun-lock' enemies and use this extra time to have heroes with stress-healing abilities restore the party's stress. However, by doing so, the player runs the risk of encountering enemy [reinforcements](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Reinforcements), as more enemies will appear if the party lingers with a single enemy for too long.

## Preventing Stress

- Many [quirks](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Quirk) and [trinkets](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trinkets) can reduce the stress suffered by a hero, both generally and under certain circumstances.
- Many [camping skills](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Camping_skills) can [buff](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Buff) heroes by reducing the amount of stress suffered for several battles, or heal certain amounts of stress directly. In particular, Encourage and Pep Talk are camping skills which heals stress and buffs stress reduction, respectively, and may be learned by all Heroes, except for the Flagellant.
- Many [Curios](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Curio) can heal stress, usually when interacted with using a particular supply item with the right object, such as the Confession Booth and Holy Water.
- Traveling at higher [light levels](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Light_meter) provides a blanket percentage reduction on Stress inflicted.
- [Scouting](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouting) indirectly prevents stress by allowing detection of [traps](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Trap), granting a chance to avoid or disarm them. Successfully disarming a trap heals stress as well (8).
- Quickly eliminating stress-focused [enemies](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies) such as the [Swine Wretch](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Swine_Wretch) or [Cultist Acolyte](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Cultist_Acolyte) can greatly reduce the amount of stress you incur in the long run.
- Killing any enemy has 50% chance to heal a small amount of stress to the killing hero (3). A hero performing a [critical hit](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Critical_Hit) on an active enemy will heal a small amount of stress as well (3). These effects may stack. Critical hits also grant other party members a 25% chance each of receiving a small stress heal as well (3). Critical heals also provide a slight stress relief (3) to a healed hero.
- Some [Heroes](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heroes) ' combat skills can heal stress for themselves (the [Abomination](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Abomination) 's "Absolution" or [Leper](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Leper) 's "Solemnity"), or others ( [Crusader](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Crusader) 's "Inspiring Cry" or [Jester](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Jester) 's "Inspiring Tune"). In addition, Crusader, Jester, and [Houndmaster](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Houndmaster) have access to trinkets which increase the amount of stress healed with their skills.
- Any hero who becomes [Virtuous](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtue) goes from 100 Stress down to 45, and grants a small stress heal (6) to all other members of the party. Certain Virtuous states will also grant the Virtuous hero a continuous stress resistance buff or actively heal the stress of either the Virtuous hero (15) or others (3).
- Inversely, heroes who becomes [Afflicted](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Affliction) will inflict stress on other party members, and will continue to do so as long as they are afflicted. Avoiding Affliction is paramount to avoiding large amounts of stress in the long run.
- Using heroes infected with the [Crimson Curse](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Crimson_Curse) who are Craving and Wasting to interact with Curios can prevent stress damage, as these heroes can deal stress damage when other heroes interact with Curios.
- In all states of Crimson Curse (except Passive), heroes can inflict stress on allies in the same vein as Afflicted ones, although heroes in Bloodlust state also have a chance to heal their own stress (15) at the start of their turn.
- Sources that reduce the amount of stress damage received cannot exceed reducing this by 80%.

## [Butcher's Circus](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Butcher%27s_Circus)

In this PvP game mode, several of the heroes' skills have been reworked to always induce Stress or inflict Horror, allowing for a Stress-focused party to function. Stress-based parties focus on a battle of attrition, aiming to get every enemy Afflicted before pushing them into suffering from [Heart attacks](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Heart_attack) repeatedly until they succumb. Some variations combine stress with Bleeds and Blights, and use those ailments to push the opposition to Death's Door repeatedly for extra Stress and greater chances at inducing heart attacks.
