⚠️ The information in this article is from game version 1.4.6 on Steam.

## General Information!

**[Passive Items](#Passive_Items)** help the player by giving them various bonuses. There are many synergies between items and even [Item Sets](https://neonabyss.fandom.com/wiki/Item_Sets).

**[Temporary Items](#Temporary_Items)** don't provide any bonuses, but rather act as "keys" to certain floors. They will hover behind the player like pets or eggs once picked up.

**[Cosmetic Items](#Cosmetic_Items)** are firstly added in the game version 1.4.4 (Halloween) on Steam. They don't provide any bonuses.
